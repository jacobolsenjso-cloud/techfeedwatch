### Precision Diagnostics and Predictive Maintenance

One of AI's most impactful contributions to the repair industry is its ability to analyze vast datasets, identify patterns, and offer unparalleled diagnostic accuracy.
-   **Faster Fault Detection:** AI algorithms can quickly pinpoint the root cause of a device malfunction, even in complex "combo change" scenarios where multiple components might be failing. This reduces diagnostic time and human error.
-   **Predictive Analytics:** Beyond reactive repair, AI is enabling **predictive maintenance**. By analyzing usage data and historical failure rates, AI can forecast when a component is likely to fail, allowing for proactive replacements before total breakdown. Imagine a system that tells you a device's battery and display are likely to fail in tandem, prompting a pre-emptive "combo change" that saves the user downtime.
