## The Unseen Giant: Engineering Marvels

The B-2 Spirit's silhouette is instantly recognizable, yet its true scale and complexity are often underestimated. Its design is unlike any other bomber, optimized for stealth and aerodynamic efficiency.

-   **Wingspan:** A staggering **172 feet (52.4 meters)**, giving it an imposing presence in the sky. This massive span contributes to its stability and lift, crucial for its long-range missions.
-   **Length:** A compact **69 feet (21 meters)**, a deceptive dimension given its wingspan, highlighting its unique "flying wing" design.
-   **Max Takeoff Weight:** Weighing in at an immense **336,500 lbs (152,635 kg)** when fully loaded, the B-2 is a true heavy-lifter. This weight capacity is a testament to its robust airframe and powerful engines, enabling it to carry vast amounts of fuel and ordnance.

## Speed, Range, and Global Reach

What truly sets the B-2 apart is its ability to project power globally, swiftly and discreetly.

-   **Max Speed:** Operating at **Mach 0.95 (Subsonic high-speed)**, the B-2 can traverse vast distances efficiently without attracting undue attention from sonic booms.
-   **Unrefueled Range:** An astonishing **6,000 nautical miles** without requiring refueling. This capability means the B-2 can launch from bases in the United States, strike targets almost anywhere on Earth, and return, all without touching down. This unparalleled range offers immense strategic flexibility.
