## AR vs. Mixed Reality: Understanding the Innovation

While often used interchangeably, there's a crucial distinction between Augmented Reality (AR) and Mixed Reality (MR) that AR Engineering masterfully employs.

-   **Augmented Reality (AR)**: This technology overlays digital information, such as 3D models or instructional graphics, onto the real world. Think of an app that lets you preview furniture in your living room or a heads-up display on a windshield.
-   **Mixed Reality (MR)**: MR takes AR a significant step further. It blends virtual and real worlds to create new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real time. For AR Engineering, this means users can not only see a virtual engine part but also manipulate it as if it were physically present, receiving instant feedback within their actual environment. This capability is critical for complex engineering systems and advanced simulations, allowing for a truly integrated learning experience.
