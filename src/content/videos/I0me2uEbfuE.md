### The Pillars of AI: Understanding Core Model Types

While the video covers a spectrum of models, certain categories represent the most impactful advancements:

*   **Generative AI:** Models capable of creating new content—text, images, audio, and more—from simple prompts. Think of tools like Midjourney or stable diffusion for images, or advanced LLMs for text.
*   **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Sophisticated AI systems trained on massive datasets of text, enabling them to understand, generate, and process human language with remarkable fluency. Examples include OpenAI's **ChatGPT**, Google's Gemini, and Anthropic's Claude.
*   **Specialized AI:** Models designed for particular tasks, such as predictive analytics, image recognition, natural language processing (NLP), or even optimizing [AI-Enhanced SEO: The Free Traffic Hack Your Website Needs Now!](/video/ud0Ne7efqMU). These often form the backbone of industry-specific applications.
