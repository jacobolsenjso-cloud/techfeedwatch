Demis Hassabis, CEO of Google DeepMind, recently outlined a long-term vision for Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) that extends far beyond current machine learning applications. His insights emphasize AI's capability not just to process data, but to fundamentally understand and simulate complex realities, from biological systems to the laws of physics.

DeepMind, known for its work with AlphaGo and AlphaFold – which earned Hassabis a Nobel Prize for its protein structure prediction breakthrough – is now focused on the foundational challenges for AGI. This includes exploring how AI can discern "learnable patterns" in nature and tackle complex computational problems. The aspiration is to move towards systems capable of general scientific discovery and understanding, a significant leap from specialized AI.
