## Implications for the Construction Workforce

The introduction of such an efficient robotic mason inevitably raises questions about the future of human labor in construction. Will masons become obsolete? The answer, while complex, points towards evolution rather than outright replacement. Just as industrial revolutions created new jobs while transforming old ones, AI in construction will likely lead to new roles focused on:

*   **Machine operation and maintenance:** Skilled technicians to program, monitor, and service these complex machines.
*   **Design and planning:** Architects and engineers leveraging AI tools for advanced structural design and site optimization.
*   **Specialized craftsmanship:** Human masons might focus on intricate, custom projects or restoration work that requires an artistic touch beyond current AI capabilities.
