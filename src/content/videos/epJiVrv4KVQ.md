### How the Tool Works: Unpacking Precision Control

This ingenious tool transforms raw video footage into two critical data streams:
- An **OpenPose skeleton**: Capturing the precise skeletal movements of subjects.
- A **depth map (Video Depth Anything)**: Providing detailed information about the scene's spatial arrangement.

By "baking" these elements, the tool empowers users to exert unprecedented control over their AI video generations. Imagine the ability to dictate not just what happens, but *exactly how* characters move and interact within a 3D space.
