The US political landscape is seeing renewed momentum toward establishing clear federal rules for digital assets, with the Financial Innovation and Technology for the 21st Century Act (FIT21) advancing through Congress. This legislative effort marks a significant step towards resolving years of regulatory ambiguity that have clouded the crypto market. The bill primarily seeks to define the jurisdiction of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) over digital assets, an issue that has led to numerous enforcement actions and market uncertainty.

For years, the absence of a cohesive framework has placed the US at a disadvantage compared to regions with clearer guidelines, such as the European Union's Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation. Clear rules could foster innovation within the US, rather than pushing it abroad.

Beyond FIT21, broader crypto regulation efforts also include discussions around stablecoin legislation, another critical component for integrating digital assets into the mainstream financial system.
