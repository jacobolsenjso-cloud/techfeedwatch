Many AI-generated websites today share a common, often uninspired aesthetic, frequently described as "AI slop." This perception suggests that while AI tools like Claude Code are adept at constructing functional website frameworks, they often fall short in delivering visual distinction and professional polish. However, this outcome is not inevitable; a strategic approach blending AI's generative power with targeted human design expertise can transform generic AI output into compelling, aesthetically pleasing digital experiences.

When a user asks how to make Claude Code websites look better, the straightforward answer lies in a two-pronged strategy: meticulous pre-generation guidance and comprehensive post-generation refinement. This involves moving beyond basic prompts to establish a clear design vision, then systematically auditing and improving the AI's initial output. The goal is to leverage Claude Code for its speed and foundational capabilities while injecting the critical human element that raises a merely functional site to one that is visually impressive and unique. ## Establishing Design Intent Before the Code

The journey to a beautiful Claude Code website begins long before the AI generates its first line of code. The initial step demands a strategic shift from generic requests to highly specific instructions that embed a clear design intent into the AI's brief. Nic Conley, for instance, emphasizes that "Most AI generated websites I see these days look like AI slop," but stresses that "they don't have to." He identifies two core "tricks" he uses to craft appealing sites with Claude Code. The first involves laying down solid groundwork through a "Claude MD Brief." This briefing goes beyond mere content outlines; it establishes the aesthetic parameters the AI should follow.

A critical part of this pre-generation phase involves finding "Design Inspiration." Instead of letting Claude Code default to generic templates, designers should supply visual cues and examples that convey the desired mood, style, and layout. This could involve mood boards, links to existing websites with preferred aesthetics, or detailed descriptions of color palettes and typographical choices. By providing specific examples, the AI gains a clearer understanding of the expected visual outcome. After gathering inspiration, the next practical step is to "Set Up Project Folder." This organizational effort, though seemingly minor, prepares the environment for the iterative development process and helps manage assets. Only then does one "Prompt Claude Code Website Build," feeding it the carefully prepared brief and design references. This initial prompt serves as the foundation, guiding the AI to produce a more refined starting point than it would with a vague command.

## Refining the AI's Output: The Human Touch

Even with the best initial prompts, AI-generated code often requires a human touch to achieve true aesthetic excellence. This is where the second trick comes into play: systematic post-generation refinement. The AI excels at generating functional code and basic layouts, but nuances in visual hierarchy, typography, and spacing typically demand human discernment. A key step here is to "Add Front End Skill" to the process. This signifies the moment a designer or developer applies their expertise directly to the AI's output, correcting imperfections and enhancing design elements. This often involves tweaking CSS, HTML, or JavaScript to align the site with the specific design vision.

The refinement process formally begins with a "First Build Review," a critical examination of the AI's initial output against the design brief and inspirations. This review is not just about functionality, but primarily about aesthetics. Following this, a "Run a Design Audit" systematically evaluates various design elements. This audit identifies areas where the AI's generation falls short, such as inconsistent spacing, awkward element placement, or suboptimal font choices. One common area of improvement is typography; the process calls to "Fix Fonts with References" and "Choose Better Google Fonts." Generic font pairings often contribute significantly to the "AI slop" look. By selecting specific, aesthetically pleasing fonts from Google Fonts and ensuring their consistent application, the visual professionalism of the site dramatically improves. Once these design refinements are made, version control becomes important. The practice of "Commit to GitHub" ensures that all changes are tracked and can be reverted if necessary, facilitating collaborative development and maintaining a clear history of the project's evolution. Finally, deploying the refined website often involves a "Deploy with a VPS" (Virtual Private Server) for hosting. This structured approach, integrating human oversight and specific front-end skills, ensures the Claude Code output transcends mere functionality to deliver a truly beautiful and distinctive website. As Nic Conley emphasizes, these "two tricks" are essential for building sites that genuinely impress.

## Where This Lands

The notion that AI-generated websites must inherently look uninspired is a misconception that stems from an incomplete understanding of how to effectively integrate AI into the design workflow. Claude Code, like other advanced AI tools, offers an unparalleled ability to rapidly prototype and generate the underlying structure of a website. However, its strength lies in automation, not necessarily in nuanced aesthetic judgment or innovative design flair. The critical distinction for achieving visually superior websites lies in the purposeful application of human design intelligence.

For a Claude Code website to truly "look better," it requires a collaborative dynamic where the AI serves as a powerful assistant, guided and refined by human expertise. This means moving beyond simple prompts and engaging with the generative process as a designer, not merely a user. This approach mandates providing rich design context upfront, leveraging specific front-end skills to refine the AI's output, and conducting rigorous design audits. Ultimately, the quality of a Claude Code website is not solely a reflection of the AI's capabilities, but rather proof of the sophistication of the human-AI interaction. Those who master this synergy will produce websites that are not only functional and efficient but also visually striking and distinct, moving far beyond the common "AI slop" and setting a new standard for AI-assisted web development.
