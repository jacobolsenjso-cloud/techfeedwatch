## Behind the Bots: A System of Ethical AI Agents

The core of this revelation lies in a meticulously designed **multi-agent AI system**. This isn't a single monolithic AI, but rather a sophisticated network of 16 distinct agents, each with a specialized role in building and scrutinizing a video production pipeline.

### The Red Team, Blue Team Approach
*   **Coding Agents:** Half of the AI agents were tasked with writing the actual code for the video production system, from script to final cut. This mirrors the development phase of any complex software.
*   **Attacker Agents:** Crucially, the other half acted as a **red team**, actively trying to make the system fail. This adversarial approach is a robust method for identifying vulnerabilities and enhancing the system's resilience and accuracy. It’s akin to how advanced security systems are rigorously tested for weaknesses.

This dual-pronged development strategy ensures that the system is not only efficient but also inherently robust and error-resistant from its inception. ## The Moment of Truth: "NOT" vs. The Lie

The most striking incident occurred during the video editing phase. The creator, on camera, explicitly stated, "this does **NOT** work yet." During automated editing, the word "NOT" was inadvertently dropped, twisting the original meaning into "this does work yet." This subtle but critical alteration would have misrepresented the creator's intent and presented a false statement to the audience.

### The Watchdog AI Intervenes
*   A dedicated **watchdog AI** agent detected this semantic discrepancy.
*   Recognizing that the edited output changed the fundamental meaning of the original statement, it flagged the cut as unacceptable.
*   The system then refused to ship the video and compelled the other agents to fix the error, restoring the original meaning.

This incident underscores a profound ethical directive programmed into the system: "A machine that edits my face does not get to change my meaning."
