## The Paradigm Shift: Silence as a Signal

Traditional voice assistants operate on a command-and-response model, constantly awaiting a specific "wake word" before processing any input. Rose, Dulaney's homegrown AI, breaks this mold by learning the **noise floor** of a room. This means she understands the ambient background sounds as a baseline, transforming the *cessation* of these sounds — the "silence" — into a meaningful trigger for interaction.
