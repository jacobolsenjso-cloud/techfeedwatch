The Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) is its runtime environment, where smart contracts—pieces of code—are executed. These contracts are immutable once deployed and automatically execute when predefined conditions are met. Instead of miners competing to solve complex puzzles (Proof-of-Work), validators stake their ETH as collateral and are chosen pseudo-randomly to create new blocks. This transition aimed to enhance security, reduce energy consumption, and set the stage for future scalability improvements, such as sharding.
