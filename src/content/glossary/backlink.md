---
term: "Backlink"
slug: "backlink"
category: "SEO"
definition: "A backlink is a hyperlink from one website to a page on another website."
---

A backlink represents an incoming hyperlink from one external website to a specific page on another website. Search engines interpret these links as votes of confidence or endorsements for the linked content, making them a fundamental component of search engine optimization (SEO). When a credible external site points to your content, it signals to search engines that your page offers valuable, relevant, and authoritative information. This process directly influences a website's position in search engine results pages, as search algorithms often prioritize sites with a strong profile of quality backlinks. A significant backlink profile not only improves organic search rankings but also drives referral traffic from the linking site and enhances a website's overall domain authority. For example, consider a leading fintech news site publishing an article discussing recent trends in blockchain technology. If that article references and links directly to an analytical piece on cryptocurrency market volatility found on Tech Feed Watch, this constitutes a backlink. This external endorsement strengthens Tech Feed Watch's perceived authority and relevance within the crypto and fintech discussions, aiding its visibility in search results.
