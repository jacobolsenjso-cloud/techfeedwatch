SEO encompasses various strategies to improve a website's visibility in unpaid search engine results. These methods include technical adjustments to a site's structure, optimizing content with relevant terms, and earning credible backlinks from other sites. The goal is to make a website more discoverable to users actively searching for information, products, or services.

SEO matters because search engines are a primary way users find online content. Websites ranking higher on results pages receive a significantly larger share of clicks. This organic traffic is valuable, coming from users directly interested in the site's offerings. Increased visibility leads to better engagement, brand recognition, and conversion opportunities without direct advertising. Businesses and content creators rely on search engines to connect with their target audiences.

For instance, a new online store selling handmade jewelry would research keywords like "unique silver necklaces" or "custom gemstone rings." They would then create product descriptions and blog posts incorporating these terms, ensure their website loads quickly, and acquire mentions from reputable craft blogs. When a potential customer searches for "unique silver necklaces," the store's website appearing on the first page of results significantly increases the likelihood of a visit and a sale.
