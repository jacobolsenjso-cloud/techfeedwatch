# Contact Us

Thanks for your interest in Tech Feed Watch. We genuinely value hearing from our readers, and every message helps make the site better.

## Email

The best way to reach us is by email:

**info@techfeedwatch.com**

We read every message and aim to reply within a few business days.

## What you can reach out about

- **Feedback and suggestions** — ideas for how we can make the site more useful.
- **Corrections** — if you spot a factual error in an article, please let us know and we'll review and fix it.
- **Topic or video requests** — a video, tool, or subject in AI, tech, fintech, or crypto that you think deserves coverage.
- **Business and advertising** — partnership, sponsorship, or advertising enquiries.
- **Privacy and data requests** — anything related to our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## About Tech Feed Watch

Tech Feed Watch is an independent tech media project that turns the best AI, technology, fintech, and crypto videos into fast, readable analysis. You can read more about who we are and how the site works on our [About page](/about).
